Monolith vs. composable

One product. Many teams. No more release train.

One big release

  1. Every team waits for the slowest
  2. One bad change blocks everyone
  3. Rollback reverts every team
  4. Build times grow with headcount
shared fate

Composable on Zephyr

  1. Each team ships on its own clock
  2. A bad remote rolls back alone
  3. Pin, range or tag each dependency
  4. Small builds, uploaded by diff
independent fate, one product
