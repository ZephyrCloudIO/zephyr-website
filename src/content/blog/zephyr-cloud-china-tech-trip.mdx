## Meeting the Teams Behind Module Federation and Rstack

Our first stop was Hangzhou, where we visited the part of [ByteDance](https://www.bytedance.com/) working on [Module Federation](https://module-federation.io/) and the broader [Rstack](https://github.com/rstackjs) ecosystem.

Rstack brings together projects including [Rspack](https://rspack.dev/), [Rsbuild](https://rsbuild.dev/), and a growing collection of tools focused on making modern web development faster and more productive. These technologies are closely connected to the ecosystem Zephyr Cloud serves, so meeting the people building them gave us a valuable opportunity to compare what we are seeing, exchange ideas, and discuss where deeper collaboration could take us.

We got an early look at some amazing new features coming across the Rstack family, as well as the advanced ways the team is using AI throughout its work. We also had the opportunity to share what we have been building at Zephyr Cloud and discuss what our teams will build and contribute together.
