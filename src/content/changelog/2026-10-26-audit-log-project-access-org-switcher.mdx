## Summary

This release makes audit logs more complete and easier to filter, tightens what project viewers and organization viewers can access, and makes it faster to switch between organizations.

## Audit Logs Capture More and Filter Correctly

Project audit logs now record the organization they belong to, and the action filters match what you select.

- Failed deployments are recorded in the audit log, so you can see attempts that did not complete.
- Denied version uploads are shown and recorded.
- Searches by log ID are validated, and the application-logs toggle behaves as expected.
- Actions taken through CI and by environment members keep the correct attribution.

## Project Access Matches Your Role

Viewers and organization viewers can no longer reach areas they are not meant to manage.

- Project viewers cannot open project settings or DevOps pages.
- Users without app edit access no longer see DevOps pages.
- Organization viewers cannot be given project editor or admin roles, or team maintainer roles.
- Changes to a project author's contributor role now save.

## Easier Project Collaborator Management

Adding people to a project works the way you would expect.

- Collaborator search finds users.
- The member and team pickers are usable and load results page by page.
- The owner no longer appears in the role list, since their access is fixed.

## Cloudflare Integrations Stay Connected

Cloudflare connections are more durable.

- Idle connections are refreshed daily, so they stay active between deploys.
- Connections that have expired can be reconnected without starting over.
- Editing an integration keeps its credentials current.

## Two-Pane Organization Switcher

The organization switcher now shows a two-pane layout with your recently used organizations, so you can jump back to the one you were working in.

## Smaller Improvements

- Team creation explains why a name is taken when a duplicate conflicts.
- The CI token notice wording matches the current token deprecation status.
- Emails use the hosted Zephyr wordmark, so the logo displays correctly.
- Ephemeral environments now follow the lifecycle of their pull request, and a pool of 20 reusable database branches is available.

## What This Means for Teams

- Audit logs give a more complete and accurate record of who did what, including failed and denied actions.
- Project and organization roles are enforced more consistently, reducing accidental access.
- Adding collaborators and switching organizations takes fewer steps.
- Cloudflare integrations need less manual reconnecting.
