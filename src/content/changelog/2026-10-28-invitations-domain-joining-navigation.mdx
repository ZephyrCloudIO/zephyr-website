## Summary

This release makes organization invitations more reliable, lets colleagues on a verified domain join without an extra step, and tidies up tables and navigation.

## Invitations Work the Way You Expect

Invitations now stay open until the person accepts them, and the role you chose applies when they do.

- Pending invitations are not cancelled or consumed before they are accepted.
- The invited role is applied when the invitation is accepted.
- Each person takes up one seat per subscription, even if they are invited more than once.

## Joining Your Organization Is Simpler

Signed-in colleagues with an email address on a verified domain can join their organization directly, without an extra accept step.

## Easier Navigation and Tables

- The Tags table grows with the page, so long lists are easier to scan.
- The DevOps section of the navigation can be collapsed, keeping the sidebar tidy.

## Smaller Improvements

- Changing a member's login email is no longer possible from the profile update flow, which keeps sign-in details protected.
- Unused profile email-options endpoints have been removed.
- The OpenAPI reference loads faster, since the document is now cached.
- The in-app chat widget has been removed.

## What This Means for Teams

- Invitations behave predictably, so people get the role you intended.
- Colleagues on a verified domain can get started faster.
- Sign-in details are better protected from accidental changes.
- The interface is tidier, especially for large tables and the DevOps area.
