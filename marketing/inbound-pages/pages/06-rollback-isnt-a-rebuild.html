Rebuild vs. repoint

The fastest rollback is one you never build.

Rollback by rebuild

  1. Find the last good commit
  2. Revert, wait for CI
  3. Build it again, hope it matches
  4. Upload and deploy, under pressure

How it works

Deploy is not release.

01Every build deploys

Each build is uploaded once and kept live at its own URL.

02Release moves a tag

production points at one build. The change takes effect without a rebuild.

03Rollback moves it back

Same move, opposite direction. The old build never left.
