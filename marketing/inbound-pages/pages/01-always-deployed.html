How it works

Three steps. No platform team.

01Add the plugin 
// vite.config.ts
plugins: [react(), withZephyr()]
// or: npx with-zephyr
02Build like you already do

Zephyr fingerprints your files and uploads only what changed. The build is live at its own URL when it finishes.

03Release when you are ready

Point production, staging or any environment at the build you want. Vite, Rsbuild, Rspack, webpack and more.
